# Privacy Policy for Open Spider Solitaire

Last updated: 2026-08-31

## We Collect Nothing

Open Spider Solitaire collects no data. Nothing is sent to us, the developers, or to
anyone else.

- No analytics or tracking
- No advertising
- No crash reporting
- No accounts
- No in-app purchases
- No permissions: the app never asks for your camera, photos, microphone, contacts,
  location, or health data, and it does not use the advertising identifier

There are no exceptions, and no optional feature that changes this.

## The App Is Entirely Offline

The app makes no network connections. It contains no networking code, no third-party
SDKs, and no analytics libraries. It works identically in airplane mode.

## Your Game Data Stays on Your Device

The app saves your unfinished game and your high scores so you can pick up where you
left off. That save never leaves your device, and we have no access to it. There is no
iCloud sync. To erase it, use Reset on the High Scores screen, or delete the app.

## Children's Privacy

We collect no data from anyone, so the app is safe for all ages, including children
under 13.

## Changes to This Policy

If our data practices ever change, we will update this policy and ship it in an app
update.
