1.0.1 — 2026-09-10
- Winning a game that makes the leaderboard now goes to High Scores, opened on the
  difficulty just played, with that game's score picked out in red. A win that misses
  the top ten goes to the title screen as before.
- Leaving a game with the back button no longer ends it. The board, score, and clock
  are kept, and the title screen offers Continue Game while one is waiting. The clock
  stops while it waits. Starting a new game asks first, and clears the kept game.
- Tapping anywhere in a column plays that column's top card, so the target is the whole
  column rather than one card — easier to hit on a phone, where ten columns leave each
  card narrower than a fingertip.
